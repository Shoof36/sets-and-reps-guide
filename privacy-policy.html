Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Sets & Reps ("the App") is operated by KAS Holding LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password through Firebase Authentication. This is used solely for account access and authentication.

User-Provided Data

You may voluntarily provide the following data within the App:

  • Name, date of birth, height, and sex (for fitness calculations)
  • Fitness goals
  • Injuries and health notes, such as recovering from a surgery or lower back issues, which you may choose to add so the AI coach can plan around them
  • Workout routines and exercise history, including your notes
  • Nutrition and food logging data, including custom foods
  • Daily body weight and step counts

AI Features

The AI fitness coach and AI calorie estimation features require you to supply your own API key for an AI provider of your choice: Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic (Claude). When you use those features, the App sends your requests from your device directly to the provider you selected, using your key. They do not pass through our servers.

So that the coach can personalize its advice, each message to the AI coach also includes information from your profile and logs, such as your name, age, height, sex, fitness goals, any injuries and health notes you added, body weight, workouts and routines, exercise notes, and food and step logs, along with short summaries of your earlier coach conversations. Coach notifications are on by default once you set up AI, and you can turn them off in Settings. Their daily message is written from a smaller summary (your name, goals, how long you have used the App, recent workouts, current body weight and its trend, and food logging streak), which never includes your injuries and health notes. It appears as a notification, which may show on your lock screen. AI calorie estimation sends only the meals you describe.

We do not store your AI conversations anywhere; they stay on your device. Your API key is held in your device's secure keystore and is never transmitted to us. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy, OpenAI's Privacy Policy, and Anthropic's Privacy Policy for how they handle data.

Voice Input

If you use the microphone to dictate, your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's, on Android) turns your speech into text, and the App receives only the text. That service's own privacy policy applies.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

On Your Device

Your data is stored on your device in a local database (Hive), so the App works without a connection.

In the Cloud

While you are signed in, your data is also stored in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your account, so it stays in step across your devices and comes back when you sign in on a new one. This includes your profile (with any injuries and health notes you add), workouts, routines, nutrition and food logs, body weight, step counts, custom foods and exercises, exercise notes, nutrition goals, and your terms acceptance status. It is secured with Firebase security rules so that only you can access your own data. Backups of the cloud database are kept for up to 7 days.

Only on Your Device

Your AI API key, your AI coach conversations, your App preferences, and a workout in progress are never uploaded.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication — account management (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google Cloud Firestore — cloud data storage (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Gemini API, OpenAI, or Anthropic — AI features, only the one you choose and only when you supply your own API key (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic)

4. Advertising

The App is free and shows interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Keep your data in step across your devices, and restore it when you sign in on a new one
  • Authenticate your account
  • Display advertisements

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the App's functionality. Information reaches an AI provider only when you use the AI features, sent directly from your device with your own API key.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for secure account access
  • Firestore security rules restricting data access to the owning user
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the data the App sends
  • Your AI API key kept in your device's secure keystore

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all of your cloud data at any time from within the App: open the Settings tab and choose Delete Account. This deletes your account and removes your data from our cloud database; backup copies are deleted automatically within 7 days. Signing out keeps your data on the device; uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it (on iPhone, a saved AI API key can remain in the device's keychain, so remove it in Settings first). You can also request deletion by contacting us at kas.holding.llc@gmail.com.
