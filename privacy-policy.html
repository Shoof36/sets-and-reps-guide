Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

Sets & Reps ("the App") is operated by KAS Holding LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password through Firebase Authentication. This is used solely for account access and authentication.

User-Provided Data

You may voluntarily provide the following data within the App:

  • Name, date of birth, height, and sex (for fitness calculations)
  • Fitness goals
  • Workout routines and exercise history
  • Nutrition and food logging data
  • Daily body weight and step counts

AI Conversations

When you use the AI fitness coach or AI calorie estimation features, your conversation messages are sent to Google's Gemini API (or OpenAI's API if you choose the BYOK option) for processing. We do not store your AI conversations on our servers. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and OpenAI's Privacy Policy for how they handle data.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Local Storage

The majority of your data (workouts, nutrition logs, body stats, routines, and preferences) is stored locally on your device using Hive, a local database. This data does not leave your device unless you choose to export it.

Cloud Storage

Certain data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore linked to your account, including your terms acceptance status. This data is secured with Firebase security rules so that only you can access your own data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication — account management (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google Cloud Firestore — cloud data storage (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google AdMob — advertising for free-tier users (Google Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • Google Gemini API — AI features (Google Privacy Policy)

4. Advertising

Free-tier users are shown interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Premium subscribers do not see ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Authenticate your account
  • Process AI fitness coaching and calorie estimation requests
  • Manage premium subscriptions
  • Display advertisements to free-tier users

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the App's functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for secure account access
  • Firestore security rules restricting data access to the owning user
  • Local-first data storage that keeps most data on your device

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. To request deletion of your cloud-stored data and account, contact us at kas.holding.llc@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
