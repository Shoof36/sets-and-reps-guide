Routines

The Routines tab is where you build and manage your workout plans.

  • Create a routine — Tap the + button to start a new routine. Give it a name and add exercises from the exercise database.
  • Edit a routine — Tap on any routine to view it, then tap the edit icon to change the name, reorder exercises, or adjust sets and reps.
  • Delete a routine — Swipe left on a routine or use the delete option inside the edit view.
  • Supersets — Group two or more exercises together as a superset. During a workout, superset exercises are presented back-to-back before the rest timer starts.
  • Start a workout — Tap the play button on a routine to begin. You'll be guided through each exercise, logging your weight and reps as you go. A rest timer runs between sets automatically.

Log

The Log tab shows your completed workout history.

  • Day view — See all workouts completed on a specific date.
  • Week view — Switch to the week view for a broader look at your training frequency.
  • Workout details — Tap any logged workout to see the full breakdown: exercises performed, sets, reps, and weight used.

Exercises

Browse and manage the full exercise database.

  • Browse by target area — Exercises are organized by muscle group (chest, back, shoulders, legs, arms, core, etc.). Tap a category to see all available exercises.
  • Exercise details — Tap an exercise to see which muscles it targets.
  • Custom exercises — Can't find what you need? Tap the + button to create a custom exercise with your own name and target area. Custom exercises work just like built-in ones.

Stats

Track your progress over time with charts and personal records.

  • Weight tracking — Log your body weight daily. View your weight trend on a chart to see how it changes over time.
  • Step tracking — Log daily step counts and view your step history on a chart.
  • Workout frequency — See how often you've been training with a workout frequency chart.
  • Personal records — The app automatically tracks your heaviest lift for each exercise. Check your PRs to see how much stronger you've gotten.

Calories

Track your daily nutrition and manage your calorie intake.

  • Profile setup — Enter your age, weight, height, sex, and activity level to calculate your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). This gives you a personalized calorie target.
  • Log food manually — Tap the + button and enter the food name, calories, protein, carbs, and fat yourself.
  • Search for food — Use the search feature to look up foods from a database and log them with one tap.
  • AI voice logging — Tap the microphone icon and describe what you ate in plain language (e.g., "two eggs and a slice of toast"). The AI estimates the macros and logs the entry for you.
  • Daily view — See all your logged meals for the day along with a chart showing your calorie and macro breakdown vs. your target.

Coach

Get personalized fitness advice from an AI coach.

  • Conversations — Start a conversation with the AI coach to ask about training programs, form tips, nutrition advice, or anything fitness-related. The coach has context about your profile and goals.
  • API keys (BYOK) — By default, the coach uses the app's built-in AI. If you prefer, you can bring your own API key (BYOK) from OpenAI by entering it in Settings. This lets you use your own account and API quota.
  • Premium — Premium subscribers get unlimited coach conversations without needing their own API key.

Settings

Customize the app to fit your preferences.

  • Theme — Switch between light and dark mode, or follow your system setting.
  • Subscription — Manage your Premium subscription to unlock ad-free usage, unlimited AI coach access, and more.
  • Rest timer — Set your default rest timer duration between sets. The timer runs automatically during workouts.
  • Daily reminders — Enable daily notifications to remind you to work out at a time you choose.
  • Coach notifications — Get motivational check-ins from the AI coach throughout the day.
  • Data import/export — Export all your data (workouts, nutrition, stats) to a file for backup. You can also import data from a previously exported file to restore or transfer your information.

Profile

Set up your personal information so the app can tailor calculations to you.

  • Name — Used to personalize your experience and AI coach interactions.
  • Birthday — Your age is used for calorie and fitness calculations.
  • Height — Used for BMI and TDEE calculations.
  • Sex — Factors into calorie and fitness formulas.
  • Fitness goals — Tell the app whether you want to lose weight, maintain, or build muscle. This adjusts your calorie targets and helps the AI coach give relevant advice.
